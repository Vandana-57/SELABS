# Smart Parking Space Rental & License Validator

## Requirements Table

| ID | Type | Description | Priority | Acceptance Criteria | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FR-001 | Functional | The system shall record vehicle entry time by scanning the vehicle number plate at the parking entrance and associate it with the vehicle's reservation. | High | Pass: Correct vehicle number and entry time are recorded. Fail: Vehicle details or entry time are incorrect or missing. | Accurate entry records are required for reservation validation and billing. |
| FR-002 | Functional | The system shall validate whether a vehicle has a valid parking reservation before allowing entry. | High | Pass: A valid reservation is accepted and entry is permitted. Fail: An invalid or expired reservation is incorrectly accepted. | Prevents unauthorized vehicles from occupying reserved parking spaces. |
| FR-003 | Functional | The system shall calculate parking charges based on the vehicle's total parking duration using progressive hourly rates. | High | Pass: The calculated fee matches the configured rate tiers and actual duration. Fail: An incorrect rate tier or duration is applied. | Ensures fair and accurate billing for vehicle owners. |
| FR-004 | Functional | The system shall record vehicle exit time and generate the final parking bill when the vehicle leaves. | High | Pass: Exit time is recorded and the correct final bill is generated. Fail: Exit time or final bill is incorrect. | Provides a complete parking transaction record and enables accurate payment processing. |
| FR-005 | Functional | The system shall flag vehicles that exceed their reserved parking duration and notify the parking warden. | Medium | Pass: Overstaying vehicles are identified and displayed to the warden. Fail: An unauthorized overstay is not detected or reported. | Helps parking wardens manage limited parking capacity and unauthorized usage. |
| NFR-001 | Performance & Security | The license plate lookup and reservation validation process shall be completed within 300 ms under normal operating conditions while protecting vehicle and reservation data from unauthorized access. | High | Pass: At least 95% of validation requests complete within 300 ms during load testing and unauthorized access attempts are rejected. Fail: Response time or access-control requirements are violated. | Fast validation prevents congestion at parking gates while security protects vehicle and reservation information. |
| NFR-002 | Reliability & Availability | The parking system shall maintain accurate transaction records and remain available during normal parking operations, including temporary network interruptions. | High | Pass: No entry or exit transaction is lost during simulated failures and the system recovers without data inconsistency. Fail: Transactions are lost, duplicated, or corrupted. | Reliable records are essential for billing, dispute resolution, and parking management. |
